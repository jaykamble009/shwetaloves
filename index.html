Dear Shweta,

Sometimes, the most beautiful connections begin in the most unexpected ways. And honestly, I never imagined that one simple friend request could bring someone so special into my life.

I still think about that day. If you hadn't sent me that friend request, maybe we would have remained complete strangers. We might never have talked, never have known each other, and never have shared the little moments that mean so much to me today.

People often say that God brings certain people into our lives for a reason. I don't know what destiny has planned for us, but sometimes I feel that meeting you wasn't just a coincidence. Maybe some connections are meant to happen, even when we least expect them.

We haven't met face to face yet, and I know there's still so much for us to discover about each other. But that doesn't change how special you've become to me.

I genuinely look forward to the day we finally meet. I imagine us talking for hours, laughing at the smallest things, teasing each other, having lots of fun, and taking countless photos together. I hope we'll create beautiful memories that we can look back on years later and smile about.

And there's something else I truly wish for.

If we decide to take this connection forward, I hope we always have trust, honesty, loyalty, and understanding between us. I hope we never let misunderstandings or anyone else's interference come between what we're building. Whatever challenges life brings, I want us to be able to talk openly, understand each other, and choose honesty over silence.

I'm not going to make unrealistic promises about a future neither of us can predict. But I can tell you that my feelings are genuine.

I want to know you better, understand your dreams, celebrate your happiness, support you through difficult days, and hopefully become someone who makes your life a little more beautiful.

I don't know exactly where our story will lead, but I would love to discover that with you, one step at a time.

And before I end this letter, there's something I've been wanting to tell you for a long time.

I LOVE YOU, SHWETA. ♥

Not because our story is perfect, but because what I feel for you is real.

And if you feel the same, I'd love for us to give this beautiful connection a chance.

With love,

Someone who hopes to make beautiful memories with you. ♥
